About Us

Founded in August 2024, The Petite Grocer, LLC is a California-based boutique grocery company offering specialty products and curated local goods. Our mission is to provide top-quality groceries with a personal touch. We operate under the leadership of Ann-Marie Knox and The Petite Grocer Corp.
